Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the Qamha Calls application (the App), operated by Creativity Linkers for Al-Qamha Factory in Baghdad, Iraq, handles information.

1. Who uses the App

The App is used only by our own employees to communicate with business customers (shops and distributors) who deal with Al-Qamha Factory. It is not offered to the general public.

2. Information we process

  • Business contact details: shop name, owner name, phone number, city and area.
  • WhatsApp messages and call metadata (time, duration, status) exchanged between our staff and the customer through the WhatsApp Business Platform.
  • Order and account records: products, quantities, payments and balances.

3. How we use it

To answer customers, take and prepare orders, follow up payments, and keep an accurate commercial record. We do not sell personal data and do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Sharing

Data is shared only with Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform) as needed to send and receive messages and calls, and with our own staff. We may disclose data if required by Iraqi law.

5. Storage and security

Data is stored on a private server operated by the company and accessed through a private encrypted network. Access is limited to authorized staff by role.

6. Retention

Commercial records are kept as long as needed for the business relationship and legal bookkeeping, then deleted or anonymized.

7. Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your data at any time. See Data Deletion Instructions or write to hassan4ever92@gmail.com.

8. Changes
